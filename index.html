ABOUT US

Sign Board Manufacturers in Kodambakkam, Chennai

Arun Plastics is a sign board and printing company on Arcot Road, Kodambakkam, Chennai. We design and manufacture LED glow sign boards, ACP 2D/3D name boards, acrylic boards, UV boards, standees, wall graphics and vehicle branding for shops, offices, clinics, showrooms and businesses of every size.
